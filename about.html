About Us

Welcome to SA SAHA CARE

At Sa Saha Care, we are committed to providing compassionate, respectful, and personalized care for individuals with Down syndrome. We believe every individual is unique, with their own abilities, interests, personality, and potential.

Our goal is to create a safe, welcoming, and inclusive environment where individuals can feel valued, supported, and confident. Through personalized care, engaging activities, skill development, social interaction, and continuous encouragement, we help individuals build greater independence and enjoy a meaningful quality of life.

At Sa Saha Care, we also believe that families and caregivers are an important part of the care journey. We work together with families to understand individual needs and provide consistent support and guidance.

We care with compassion, support with understanding, and help every individual grow with confidence.

NDIS Support

Disability Support Services with Dignity & Independence

At Sa Saha Care, we are committed to providing compassionate, personalized, and high-quality disability support services that empower individuals to live with dignity, confidence, and independence. Our dedicated team focuses on creating a safe, supportive, and inclusive environment where every individual feels valued, respected, and cared for.

We understand that every person has unique needs and abilities. Our services are designed to support individuals across different stages of life, helping them achieve their personal goals, develop everyday living skills, and enjoy a more fulfilling life. Through a person-centred approach, we strive to deliver care that reflects each individual's preferences, aspirations, and support requirements.

Our services include disability support, Supported Independent Living (SIL), Short-Term Accommodation (STA), and personalized assistance tailored to individual needs. We work closely with participants, families, and support networks to ensure that the right care and assistance are available when needed.

At Sa Saha Care, we believe that everyone deserves the opportunity to participate in their community and experience greater independence. Our goal is to build meaningful relationships, encourage personal growth, and make a positive difference in the lives of the individuals and families we support.

At Sa Saha Care, we provide compassionate, personalized care that helps individuals with Down syndrome develop confidence, independence, and essential life skills. We create a safe and inclusive environment where every individual feels respected, valued, and supported.

  • Personalized Care & Development
  • Safe, Inclusive & Family-Centered Support

Our vision is to create an inclusive community where individuals with Down syndrome have the opportunity to learn, grow, connect, and live happy and fulfilling lives. We believe every individual deserves dignity, acceptance, and the opportunity to reach their unique potential.

  • Empowering Individuals to Thrive
  • Building an Inclusive & Supportive Community
