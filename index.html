Welcome to SA Saha Care

Empowering Lives Through Inclusive Support

SA Saha Care is a dedicated disability support service in Australia, committed to helping people with disabilities live independent, meaningful, and fulfilling lives. We believe everyone deserves the opportunity to achieve their goals, participate in their communities, and feel included.

We provide personalised, person-centred support that promotes physical, emotional, and social well-being. Our compassionate and experienced team is passionate about making a positive difference through inclusive care, empowering individuals to build independence and enjoy a better quality of life.

Compassionate Care

Care That Puts You First

At SA SAHA CARE, we believe in treating individuals with mental health challenges and disabilities with dignity, respect, and compassion. We don't label people as "mad" or "broken"—we recognize them as unique individuals deserving of proper care, support, and inclusion in their communities.
