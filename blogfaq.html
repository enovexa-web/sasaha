Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is Down syndrome? +

Down syndrome is a genetic condition caused by the presence of an extra copy of chromosome 21, which can affect development and learning in different ways.

2. Who can receive support at Sa Saha Care? +

Sa Saha Care provides support for individuals with Down syndrome based on their individual needs, abilities, and care requirements.

3. What type of care does Sa Saha Care provide? +

We provide personalized care, daily support, life-skills development, and opportunities for social and recreational activities.

4. Do you provide support for daily activities? +

Yes, we can provide appropriate assistance with everyday activities and encourage individuals to develop greater independence.

5. Do you provide therapy services? +

Therapy and developmental support may be provided through qualified professionals, depending on the services available at Sa Saha Care.

6. What activities are available? +

Activities may include arts and crafts, music, dance, games, social interaction, and other recreational activities designed to encourage participation.

7. How do you support individual development? +

We focus on each individual's abilities and goals while encouraging communication, confidence, social skills, and independence.

8. Do you involve families in the care process? +

Yes, we believe family involvement is important and work closely with families and caregivers to provide consistent support.

9. Is the care personalized for each individual? +

Yes, our approach is centered on understanding each individual's needs, preferences, abilities, and level of independence.

10. How can I learn more about Sa Saha Care? +

You can contact our team to learn more about our services, care approach, availability, and how we can support your family.
